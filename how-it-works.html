Finding a room

  1. Search by campus, dates and budget.
  2. Message the host inside Rentlet.
  3. Settle the dates, what stays, money, a video tour and the landlord’s OK in the chat.

Contact details are shared only when you both agree.

Posting a room

  1. Post in about ten minutes: campus, dates, rent and deposit, photos.
  2. It goes live as soon as you publish. The map shows its exact spot; the street address stays private to you.
  3. People message you. You choose who gets the room.

From Profile you can edit a listing, mark it as taken or take it down. It leaves search after its move-out day.

Verification

What the badge means

  • They signed in with a school Google account, one with an email ending in .edu. Only those accounts can sign in.
  • Hosts can add lease proof. Only the host and the Rentlet team can open it; others see only that it was sent.

What it doesn’t mean

  • It isn’t a guarantee about the person, the room or the deal.
  • Listings aren’t reviewed before they go live.

If something looks wrong

  • Use Report on the room page or in Messages. Reports go to the Rentlet team.

Money

  • Rent and any deposit go to the host directly.
  • Rentlet never holds money and is not a broker or agent.
  • A deposit, when the host asks for one, is set by the host and shown on the listing.

Never pay before you’ve seen the lease.

Privacy

  • Others can see your first name, role, school, dates and budget.
  • A listing shows its building name, if the host added one, and its exact spot on the map. Its street address and website are seen only by the host.
  • Contact is an Instagram handle or email, never a phone number, shared only inside the conversation.
