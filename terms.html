What Rentlet is

  • Rentlet is a student class project for finding and posting sublets and lease takeovers near Northwestern.
  • It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Northwestern University.
  • Rentlet is not a broker, agent or landlord, and doesn’t act for either side of a deal.

Who can use it

  • Anyone can browse listings and requests.
  • To post, message, save or report, sign in with a Northwestern Google account (@u.northwestern.edu or @northwestern.edu).
  • Use your own account, and use Rentlet only to find or offer a place to live.

Money and leases

  • Rentlet never collects, holds or forwards rent or deposits. You pay the host, the building or the landlord directly.
  • Rentlet doesn’t write, sign or enforce leases or sublease agreements. Whether a lease allows a sublet or a transfer is between you and the landlord.

Never pay before you’ve seen the lease and know who receives the money.

What you post

  • Post only a place you have the right to sublet or hand over, with honest dates, rent, deposit and photos.
  • Photos must show the place itself.
  • What you post stays yours. By posting it, you let Rentlet show it on the site so people can find it.
  • Listings go live as soon as they’re published. Rentlet doesn’t review them first.
  • You’re responsible for your listings, requests and messages.

Not allowed

  • Fake or misleading listings
  • Scams, or asking for money before the lease is seen
  • Harassment or hateful messages
  • Sharing other people’s personal details
  • Spam and ads

Reports and removal

  • If a listing or a person looks wrong, use Report on the room page or in Messages. Reports go to the Rentlet team. Rentlet has no support email.
  • The Rentlet team may remove listings, requests or messages that break these terms, and block accounts that do.

No guarantees

  • Rentlet is a class project, offered as it is. It may change, pause or close at any time.
  • A verification badge shows what was checked, such as a Northwestern sign-in. It isn’t a guarantee about a person, a place or a deal.
  • Rentlet isn’t a party to any deal made through it and isn’t responsible for one.

Your account

  • You can delete your account at any time: Profile → Account → Delete account.
  • What Rentlet stores and who can see it is in the privacy notice.
